# TheExamQuestions: SAP Certification Guide & Practice Resources

Welcome to the official GitHub repository for **TheExamQuestions**. This space is dedicated to helping professionals and students successfully prepare for and pass their SAP certification exams. 

Whether you are navigating foundational concepts or tackling advanced technical scenarios, these resources are structured to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world exam readiness.

## 📚 What You Will Find Here

This repository serves as a directory for our comprehensive study materials. We regularly update our guides to align with the latest SAP exam syllabus and system-based assessments.

### Core SAP Certification Modules Covered:
*   **SAP S/4HANA:** Financial Accounting, Management Accounting, Sourcing and Procurement, and Sales.
*   **SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP):** Integration Suite, Extension Suite, and SAP Build.
*   **SAP SuccessFactors:** Employee Central, Recruiting, and Performance & Goals.
*   **SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC):** Predictive analytics, planning, and reporting frameworks.
*   **SAP Ariba & Procurement:** Supply chain and spend management execution.

*(Note: If you are looking for a specific module not listed above, check our main exam directory on the website.)*

## 💡 How to Use These Resources

1.  Identify your specific SAP Certification exam code (e.g., C_TS4FI_2021, C_AIG_2412).
2.  Review the syllabus and structural breakdown provided in our module guides.
3.  Test your knowledge using our timed practice exams to identify knowledge gaps before booking your certification slot.
